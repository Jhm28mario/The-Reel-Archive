# 🎞️ The Reel Archive

**A personal digital archive and collection companion for film collectors.**

The Reel Archive is a desktop application designed to help film collectors organize, catalog, and explore their physical film collections.

I've been working on **The Reel Archive for about a month**, adding collection management tools, statistics, games, awards, and support for a wide variety of film formats.

## 🎥 Supported Film Formats

The Reel Archive supports a wide range of gauges and formats, including:

* 8mm
* Super 8
* Single 8
* 9.5mm
* 16mm
* Super 16
* Ultra 16
* 17.5mm
* 28mm
* 35mm
* 35mm 3D
* 65mm
* 70mm
* 5-perf
* 8-perf
* 3D IMAX / 15-perf 70mm (15/70)

## 🎬 Features

* Film collection catalog
* Original titles
* Release dates and print dates
* Film stock information
* Runtime and footage length
* Sound information
* Country of origin
* Film condition
* Splice tracking
* Repair tracking
* Projectability information
* Vinegar syndrome tracking
* Shrinkage tracking
* Main and additional film images
* Search and filtering
* Vault Statistics
* Film Awards
* RAW and restored video links
* Print Film Tickets
* Local SQLite database

## 🗄️ Made for Film Collectors

A physical film print can have much more information than just its title.

It can have a specific film stock, print date, soundtrack, number of splices, repairs, markings, artwork, condition, and history.

The Reel Archive is designed to keep all of that information together in one place.

## 🔐 Local Collection

The Reel Archive stores collection information locally using **SQLite**.

Your film collection remains on your computer rather than requiring an online database.
